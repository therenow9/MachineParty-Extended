# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-13)

Extended 1.1.0 comes with Better Couch Lobby 1.1.0, More Cosmetics 1.1.0 and (in the `-with-GameCube` zip) GameCube Controller Support 1.1.0.

- **Saved names on the couch lobby's name screen** (Better Couch Lobby). A pencil for a new name, then your saved names, the most recently used first: pick one with up/down and A. Names can be up to 7 letters, and up to 50 are kept. Delete them in the main menu's Options, under Saved names.
- **Chinese, Simplified and Traditional.** With the game set to either, Extended's own text follows it: the couch lobby cards, The Filter's scoreboard, Duck Hunt's two-hunter reveal, split-screen labels, GO shouts and hunter HUD, and the online refusal and room messages. GameCube Controller Support's two on-screen notes too, and its adapter warning now wraps instead of running off the screen. The saved-names page in Options is in Chinese too.
- **Seven new cosmetics** (More Cosmetics): a balloon party hat, a pharaoh's headdress, a turban, a gunslinger's cowboy hat and a royal crown, red and cyan 3D glasses and night-vision goggles. The monocle is bigger, with a gold rim and a heavier chain, so it reads from a distance. 18 hats and 8 glasses in all; the online customization screen has a fifth row for them.
- **The version is `mp-extended-1.1`,** shown after the game version in the main menu's bottom corner (`v2.1.2+mp-extended-1.1`) and in the refusal messages. Players on 1.0 and 1.1 can't join each other; players without Extended still can, at 4 or fewer.

**In the minigames:**

- **Table Manners at 6 or more:** the coach's two shots in one motion, his arm swinging straight from the first player to the second, no longer back to the draw between them. At seats 5 to 8 he aims at the player in that seat, and where a player sits within his reach he draws the gun back.
- **Spine Breaker at 6 to 8:** a spider no longer trails a player who has the other spider on their back, and for a second after you throw a spider off, the other can't grab you (one thrown at you still can).
- **The Filter at 5 or 6 on the couch:** each split-screen view is framed as at 8 players, so no neighbour stands beside your console.
- **Inside Job in split screen:** each flashlight's glow around the hand casts no shadow (the beam still does), which keeps 6 and 8 players' frame rate up with every flashlight on.

**In the lobby:**

- **Bigger couch lobby cards.** The 8 player cards are drawn at 75% instead of 65%, still side by side on a 16:9 screen, and the hat, glasses and colour pickers and the ready box show their icons at 1.15 times their own size (they were drawn slightly smaller than that, which lost pixel rows).
- **Back on the name screen** (Better Couch Lobby): a pad's X, or a GameCube pad's B, takes the letters back to the saved names, and on the saved names drops you out. A keyboard player's Enter confirms the letters.
- **LEAVE with nobody past the name screen** (Better Couch Lobby): an unseated pad's back (GameCube B, other pads' X, the keyboard's X key) lights LEAVE, and back again or A presses it, so player 1 can leave without making a character first.

**Fixed:**

- **Pressing Host, Local or Customization twice in the main menu crashed the game.** It crashed as the screen faded to black, when the second press landed inside the fade (a quick double tap of A). This was the game's own bug; now only the first press counts.
- **In the couch and online lobbies, player 3's leg went into the chair of the second row's middle seat** (seat 6). That seat and its chair now sit a little nearer the camera.
