Hat gib

Knocked off: in flight and on the floor

Minigames throw the hat as a rigid body whose only collider is a sphere of radius 0.5, the same for every hat. A hat bigger than the sphere dips through the floor; a smaller one rests on the sphere and floats. Each hat here is thrown by the game's own code with a fixed seed, over a floor at the character's feet. The numbers track how far the mesh dips under that floor every physics frame from 1 s to 4 s. Sideways speed was cut to a quarter so the hat stays in the seat's light; the spin and the fall are the game's.
