# Parked items

Items cut from More Cosmetics before a release. Their files are kept here so they can come back; the release zips leave this folder out, and the catalog doesn't list them.

| Item | Files | Cut | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| A gunslinger's cowboy hat (`xc_cowboy_hat`) | `xc_cowboy_hat.glb`, `xc_cowboy_hat.png`; generator `../xc_cowboy_hat.py` | Before 1.1.0 | It read too close to the game's own sergeant hat. The Inuit snow goggles took its place in 1.1.0. |

To bring one back, move its files into `mods/more_cosmetics/items/` and add it to `mods/more_cosmetics/catalog.gd`.
